3-Day Gorilla Trekking in Bwindi

Experience Gorilla Trekking with Isha Tours and Travel

Overview

Embark on a once-in-a-lifetime adventure with Isha Tours and Travel to meet the magnificent mountain gorillas in their natural rainforest habitat. This 3-day journey to Bwindi Impenetrable National Park or Mgahinga Gorilla National Park takes you deep into Uganda’s lush southwest, home to nearly half of the world’s remaining mountain gorillas. Led by expert guides, this safari promises an intimate, unforgettable connection with nature and wildlife.

Package Duration
3 Days / 2 Nights
Destinations
Bwindi Impenetrable Forest or Mgahinga Gorilla National Park
Ideal For
Adventure seekers, wildlife enthusiasts, and nature lovers
Gorilla Permit Fee
$800 per person (included in package)

Day 1: Travel to Bwindi Impenetrable Forest

Begin your adventure with an early morning departure from Kampala (or Entebbe). The scenic drive takes you through the rolling hills of Western Uganda, fertile farmlands, and charming roadside communities.

Enjoy lunch en route and take the opportunity to stop for photographs as you pass through Kabale or Kisoro, known for their breathtaking landscapes.

Upon arrival at Bwindi, check in at your lodge — options range from luxury, mid-range, to budget-friendly accommodations, all nestled within or near the rainforest.

Spend the evening relaxing and preparing for the thrilling trek ahead. Tonight, you’ll fall asleep surrounded by the mysterious sounds of the African jungle — home to half of the world’s endangered mountain gorillas.

Accommodation Options:

  • Luxury: Clouds Mountain Gorilla Lodge / Buhoma Lodge
  • Mid-range: Ichumbi Gorilla Lodge / Bakiga Lodge
  • Budget: Broadbill Forest Camp / Agape Guest House Kisoro

Day 2: Gorilla Trekking Experience

Rise early for breakfast before heading to the park headquarters for a briefing from the Uganda Wildlife Authority (UWA) rangers. You’ll be assigned a gorilla family group to track and begin your trek through thick rainforest terrain.

The trek can last anywhere from 2 to 6 hours, depending on the location of the gorillas, and it’s led by experienced rangers who will guide you safely through the forest.

Once you locate your assigned gorilla family, spend a magical hour observing these gentle giants — watching them feed, play, and interact in their natural environment. It’s an awe-inspiring, humbling experience you’ll remember for a lifetime.

After your trek, return to the lodge for a well-deserved rest and a hearty lunch. Spend the evening relaxing or taking a short community walk to learn about local cultures, crafts, and conservation efforts.

Overnight: Same lodge as previous night

Day 3: Return to Kampala or Entebbe

After breakfast, begin your return journey to Kampala or Entebbe.

Enjoy scenic views along the way and stop for lunch in Mbarara or Igongo Cultural Centre, where you can sample Ankole cuisine and shop for souvenirs.

Arrive in the evening, marking the end of your unforgettable gorilla trekking adventure with Isha Tours and Travel.

Package Highlights

  • Exclusive gorilla trekking experience in Bwindi or Mgahinga
  • Expert-guided rainforest hikes
  • Scenic countryside drives through southwestern Uganda
  • Cultural stopovers in Kabale, Kisoro, and Mbarara
  • Comfortable accommodations to suit every traveler’s budget

Included in Package

  • Gorilla Trekking Permit ($800 value)
  • Accommodation (2 nights)
  • All meals during the trip
  • Professional English-speaking driver/guide
  • Ground transportation (Safari Landcruiser or tour van)
  • Bottled drinking water
  • Park entrance fees

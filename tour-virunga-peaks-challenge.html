Virunga Peaks Challenge

7 Days of Hiking, Culture & Coffee

Embark on an exhilarating one-week adventure through the Virunga Highlands of southwestern Uganda. The Virunga Peaks Challenge is a tri-summit hiking expedition across Mount Gahinga, Mount Muhabura, and Mount Sabinyo, interspersed with restful and cultural experiences including coffee tours, canoeing, and traditional village visits. This package perfectly balances adventure, cultural immersion, and relaxation — ideal for thrill seekers and explorers.

Day 1: Arrival in Kisoro – Gateway to the Virunga Mountains

Meet and greet at Kigali or Entebbe Airport and transfer to Kisoro, driving through scenic hills and terraced slopes. Stop for panoramic photos of the 'Switzerland of Africa'. Evening briefing over dinner at your lodge.

Day 2: Mount Gahinga Hike – The Warm-Up Climb

After breakfast, transfer to Mgahinga Gorilla National Park. Begin your first ascent — Mount Gahinga (3,474m), a moderate 6-hour trek through bamboo forests. Spot golden monkeys along the trail. Descend and relax at your lodge.

Day 3: Cultural & Coffee Experience – The Rest Day

Visit a local coffee farm for a hands-on experience — from picking and roasting to brewing your own cup of Kigezi coffee. Later, immerse in Kigezi traditions through music, dance, and crafts. Optional canoeing or ziplining in nearby lakes. Evening bonfire and relaxation.

Day 4: Mount Muhabura Hike – The Summit of Fire

Begin your ascent of Mount Muhabura (4,127m) — the tallest of the three peaks. This challenging 8–9 hour trek rewards you with stunning views of the Virunga Volcanoes and a crater lake at the summit. Return to Kisoro for a restful evening.

Day 5: Relaxation & Lake Bunyonyi Canoeing

Transfer to Lake Bunyonyi, known for its tranquility and 29 islands. Enjoy a peaceful canoe ride, optional swimming, or kayaking. Leisurely lunch and sunset relaxation at your lodge overlooking the lake.

Day 6: Mount Sabinyo Hike – The Triple Border Peak

Your final climb — Mount Sabinyo (3,669m). Trek up rugged ridges to the point where Uganda, Rwanda, and DR Congo meet. Stand in three countries at once and take in breathtaking 360° views. Evening return to Kisoro for a celebration dinner.

Day 7: Kisoro Market Visit & Departure

Explore the colorful Kisoro Market filled with crafts, baskets, and fresh produce. Begin your return journey to Kigali or Entebbe, concluding your Virunga Peaks Challenge adventure.

Package Inclusions:

  • Hiking permits for Gahinga, Muhabura & Sabinyo
  • All park entrance fees
  • Accommodation (budget, midrange, or luxury options)
  • Professional mountain guides and rangers
  • 4x4 Safari Landcruiser with driver-guide
  • Cultural and coffee experience fees
  • All meals as per itinerary
  • Airport transfers
  • Certificate of completion for the Virunga Peaks Challenge

Trip Vibe:

A perfect blend of adventure, culture, and relaxation. For those who love the thrill of summiting peaks but also appreciate moments of quiet beauty — the Virunga Peaks Challenge offers both adrenaline and authenticity. Climb. Connect. Conquer.

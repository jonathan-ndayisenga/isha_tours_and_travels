About Isha Tours and Travels

Authentic adventures designed by locals — wildlife, culture, and nature

Who We Are

Isha Tours and Travels is an immersive travel company based in Uganda that specializes in authentic, off-the-beaten-path adventures. From gorilla tracking and golden monkey treks to cultural experiences and scenic nature tours, we design journeys that go deeper than the average safari.

Whether you’re traveling solo, as a couple, with friends, or as a family, we tailor each itinerary to match your pace, interests, and comfort level.

Our Mission: To create meaningful travel experiences that connect explorers with Uganda’s vibrant culture, breathtaking landscapes, and remarkable wildlife — all while promoting responsible and sustainable tourism.

Our Story

Founded with a passion for showcasing Uganda’s incredible beauty, Isha Tours and Travels creates immersive journeys for travelers from around the world. Our story began with a simple belief: the best trips are the ones that connect you with nature, culture, and community.

Over the years, we’ve built strong relationships with local communities and tourism partners to ensure each experience is authentic, safe, and responsibly run — benefiting people and places.

Today, we’re proud to guide guests through Uganda with warmth, professionalism, and the kind of local insight that turns a trip into a lifelong memory.
